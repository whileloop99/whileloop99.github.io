# PRIVACY POLICY
**Last Updated:** July 7, 2026

The **My Walking Pad** application ("Application") is developed to provide remote control services for smart walking pads. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Application on the Android operating system.

---

### 1. BOTTOM LINE UP FRONT (BLUF) – CORE SUMMARY
* **Information Collection:** The Application *does not* collect any Personally Identifiable Information (PII). We only require Bluetooth and Location permissions to connect to your walking pad device, and anonymous technical data is processed via third-party advertising networks.
* **Target Audience:** The Application is intended for users aged 8 and above. Ad delivery for children is strictly configured in compliance with child privacy protection standards (COPPA and GDPR-K).
* **Your Rights:** You retain full control to deny hardware permissions or opt-out of ad personalization through your Android device settings.

---

### 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT AND WHY

#### a. Android Device Permissions
To enable core functionalities and interact with your hardware, the Application requires the following permissions:
* **Bluetooth Permissions (`BLUETOOTH_SCAN`, `BLUETOOTH_CONNECT`):** Required to discover, pair, and transmit operational commands from your smartphone to the smart walking pad.
* **Location Permissions (`ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`, `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`):** Mandatory by the Android OS to perform Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) scanning. We *do not* track, store, or utilize your actual geographical location for any other purpose.

#### b. Automatically Collected Data by Third Parties (Advertising)
The Application integrates third-party advertising services (e.g., Google AdMob). These networks may automatically process certain non-personal technical identifiers, including:
* Android Advertising ID.
* Device hardware specifications (model, Android OS version).
* App performance logs (crash reports, system response times).

---

### 3. ADVERTISING AND CHILDREN'S PRIVACY POLICY

Since our Application is accessible to children and contains ads, we strictly adhere to children's online privacy regulations (COPPA and GDPR):

* **Non-Personalized Ads:** For users identified or flagged by the system as under the age of 13 (or the applicable age threshold in your jurisdiction), the Application requests the ad network to serve *non-personalized ads* only. These ads are contextual rather than based on user tracking or behavioral profiling.
* **Content Filtering:** All advertisements displayed within the Application are restricted using strict family-safe filters, blocklisting any content that is sensitive, violent, or inappropriate for minors.

---

### 4. DATA SHARING AND DISCLOSURE
We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal data to outside parties. Anonymous technical data is only processed by Google-certified advertising partners to optimize delivery and system infrastructure.

---

### 5. DATA SECURITY
We implement appropriate technical security measures to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction of device logs. Since the Application operates primarily offline via direct smartphone-to-hardware Bluetooth linkage, internet data exposure is minimized.

---

### 6. USER RIGHTS AND CHOICES
You maintain complete authority over how the Application interacts with your device:
* **Revoking Permissions:** You can disable Bluetooth and Location access at any time via *Settings > Apps > Remote Walking Pad* on your Android device. *(Note: This will disable the walking pad control functionality).*
* **Opting out of Ad ID Tracking:** You can reset or opt-out of ad personalization by navigating to *Settings > Google > Ads* on your Android device.

---

### 7. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect technological updates or regulatory amendments. Any revisions will be posted directly on this page with an updated modification date.

---

### 8. CONTACT US
If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the support email listed on the Application’s Google Play Store page.
